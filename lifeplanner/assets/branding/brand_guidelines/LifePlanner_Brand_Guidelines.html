Brand Guidelines & Identity System

Brand Essence

LifePlanner empowers adults seeking to live fuller, conscious, and intentional lives. We promote individual, couple, and group growth by recommending areas of focus and tracking meaningful progress.

Professional

Clean, trustworthy design that inspires confidence in personal growth journey

Warm

Approachable and welcoming, creating a safe space for vulnerability and growth

Minimalist Zen

Uncluttered, peaceful aesthetic that promotes mindfulness and focus

Intentional

Every design element serves a purpose in supporting conscious living

Logo System

Rainbow Logo (Primary)
Our primary brand identity featuring vibrant, warm colors that represent the full spectrum of life experiences and growth opportunities.
Logo1 (Secondary)
Our secondary brand identity providing a clean alternative for different contexts, campaigns, or when a more subtle approach is needed.

Logo Usage Guidelines

✓ DO
  • Use Rainbow Logo as primary choice
  • Maintain clear space around logo
  • Use high-resolution files
  • Preserve original proportions
  • Use Logo1 for secondary applications
  • Test readability at small sizes
✗ DON'T
  • Stretch or distort the logo
  • Change colors or apply filters
  • Add drop shadows or effects
  • Use low-resolution images
  • Place on busy backgrounds
  • Rotate or skew the logo

Caption Text - Supporting information and metadata (0.9em, 400 weight)

Typography Guidelines

  • Hierarchy: Use consistent font weights to establish clear information hierarchy
  • Readability: Maintain adequate line spacing (1.6) for comfortable reading
  • Accessibility: Ensure sufficient color contrast for all text elements
  • Responsive: Scale typography appropriately across different screen sizes

  • Maintain consistent interactions
