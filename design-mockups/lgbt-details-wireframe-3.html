Drag Performance Brunch LGBTQ+

📝 Event Description

Weekly drag performances with brunch at Toronto's iconic LGBTQ+ bookstore. Join us every Sunday for fabulous drag performances, delicious brunch menu, and a welcoming community atmosphere.


What to expect: Professional drag performances, brunch menu with vegetarian options, inclusive environment, gender-neutral facilities, wheelchair accessible venue.


Getting started: No registration required - just drop in! Bring your appetite and enthusiasm. All skill levels welcome, perfect for both newcomers and regular attendees.
