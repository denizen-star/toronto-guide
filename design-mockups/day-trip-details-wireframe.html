Niagara Wine & Culinary Adventure

Experience the finest wines and culinary delights in Ontario's premier wine region, featuring award-winning wineries, gourmet restaurants, and breathtaking vineyard views.

Distance
130 km
Duration
Full Day
Best Season
Spring-Fall
Rating
★★★★★

Why This Trip is Special

Unique Features: Award-winning wineries, gourmet farm-to-table dining, scenic vineyard tours, and exclusive wine tastings with expert sommeliers.
Special Events: Seasonal wine festivals, harvest celebrations, and culinary workshops throughout the year.
